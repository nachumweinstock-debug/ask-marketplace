Frequently asked questions

Can I take guitar lessons at Yeshiva University?

Yes. ASK lists student guitar teachers at YU for private lessons on or near campus.

What guitar styles are taught?

Acoustic, electric, classical, fingerpicking, chord-based playing, and music theory depending on the instructor.

Are guitar lessons available for complete beginners?

Yes. Many instructors specialize in teaching students who have never touched a guitar.

Do I need my own guitar for lessons?

Ideally yes, but some teachers can arrange to use their instrument for first sessions.

How much do guitar lessons cost at YU?

Typical rates are $25–$55 per session.
