Frequently asked questions

Are there personal trainers at Yeshiva University?

Yes. ASK Marketplace connects you with student personal trainers and fitness coaches at WILF and BEREN campuses.

Rates typically range from $20–$60 per session depending on the trainer and type of session.

Yes. Book individual sessions with a trainer who fits your goals and schedule.

Do campus trainers provide workout plans?

Many do. Check each trainer's listing for what they include in sessions.
