Tutor FAQ

How do I become a tutor?

Create an account, post a listing, add subjects, pricing, session type, and availability.

Can I offer online sessions?

Yes. Choose online or both when you create your listing.

How do bookings work?

Students request a time, you confirm it, and both sides can coordinate through messages.

Does Ask take a commission?

Ask Marketplace is built to help students connect. Payment details are coordinated directly unless a future payment feature is added.
